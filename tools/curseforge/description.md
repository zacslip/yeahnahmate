# Yeah Nah Mate

**Zug zug, mate.** The best part of WoW is who you play it with, so this addon is all about your guild. See your guildies on the map, find a dungeon group without leaving guild chat, plan guild nights, find out who can craft what, ask the guild a question, and give people a proper shout-out when they hit a milestone.

Made in the WoW Forever guild *Loktar Yeah Nah*, where it's community first, no drama, and a spot at the fire for everyone: min-maxing raiders, fishing enthusiasts, and anyone still getting lost in Orgrimmar. It works for any guild.

Everything is shared only with guildmates who also run the addon. Open it with **/ynm** or the minimap button.

---

## What it does

**Home**
- Your guild at a glance: who's near you right now, open groups, craft requests, the next events and any open questions.
- The officers' message and the guild Discord invite up the top.
- **Guild breakdown**: the whole guild by class, level and profession, for everyone, who's online, or who's been on this week.

**Map**
- Guildies with the addon show as class-coloured dots on your world map and minimap.
- Hover a dot for their name, level, zone and how far away they are.
- Your dot is hidden inside dungeons, and you can turn sharing off any time.

**Roster**
- Who's online, with their level, zone and what they're up to (in a group, in a dungeon, in town).
- Views for *near my level*, *in my zone* and *everyone*, with a name search and sortable columns.
- Hover a name for their rank, what they look after, their alts and their titles. One-click whisper and invite.

**Dungeons**
- Post a group with the roles you need. Guildies at the right level get a pop-up, sign up as tank, healer or DPS, and you invite them with a click.
- **I'm available**: say which roles and dungeons you'd run and for how long, and you show on everyone's list. Nobody gets left in the dungeon.
- Every Forever dungeon, in level order.

**Calendar**
- Guild events (social nights and dungeon runs; raids coming with the raid roster) with Going, Maybe or Can't, plus your role.
- A reminder 15 minutes before.
- **Important events** from the guild master and officers: a reminder everyone gets (15, 30 and/or 60 minutes before), and a heads-up when it's posted.
- **Gather**: when it's time, the organiser invites everyone who's going, one click at a time, so you all end up together.

**Professions**
- Everyone's professions and recipes in one searchable list: *who in the guild can make this?*
- **Craft requests**: post "I need 20 Wool Bandages", and guildies who know the recipe can say "I'll make it".
- A 30-day history, so everyone can see the work's shared around.

**Ask the Guild**
- Ask a question with quest, item or dungeon links, and guildies answer it. Questions take answers for 48 hours.
- The asker ticks the answers that helped, and resolved questions stay 30 days so the next person can find them.

**Legends**
- Two titles that go to whoever's helped the guild most in the last 30 days: the **Lore Master** (most answers that helped) and the **Crafty Bugger** (most craft requests made).
- Their icon shows next to their name everywhere in the addon.

**Milestones and welcomes**
- Officers set up shout-outs for when a guildie hits a level, reaches a profession skill, learns to ride, loots a legendary or earns a title. They post in guild chat, as a pop-up for guildies with the addon, or both.
- A welcome line in guild chat for new members, posted by an officer who's online with the addon.

**Proximity voice** (new)
- Hear guildies louder the closer they are, in Blizzard's own guild voice chat. No extra program: the addon just turns each guildie's volume up and down by how far away they are.
- A speaker tab on the side of the window: click it for the voice panel, right-click to turn Proximity on or off. Its colour shows where you're at: grey off, gold not in guild voice yet, green on, blue group voice.
- Pick how far voices carry (Close, Normal, Far), whether far-away guildies go silent or stay faint, and an even or natural fade. Your group is always at full volume, and you can click anyone in the list to always hear them.
- **Group voice**: in a party or raid, one click moves you to the group's own private voice chat. Guildies in the group get a pop-up to join; Back to guild voice takes you home.
- Your own volume for each guildie stays the top, mutes are left alone, and everyone's volume goes back to normal when you turn it off, leave voice or log out. Only guildies who run the addon can be placed.

**Running the guild**
- The guild master names **managers**, who get the same access in the addon.
- Pick which ranks **moderate** each tab: tidy up the calendar, close stale groups, remove old requests or questions.
- Officers edit the guild message, the Discord link and a longer Info page (rules, weekly plans) right in game.
- **Streamers**: guildies post their Twitch card and show when they're live.

## Your privacy

- Nothing leaves the game. The addon only uses the game's own hidden addon messages, to your guild or whispered to one guildmate. Nothing goes to other players, other guilds or any website.
- The first time it loads, it asks whether to share your **map position** and your **professions**. You can change either in Settings any time, and off means nothing is sent.
- No automation: every invite, whisper and sign-up is your own click. The only thing it ever says in guild chat by itself is a milestone or welcome your officers set up, and you can switch off your own milestone posts in Settings.
- Proximity voice only changes volumes on your own game, and only joins a voice channel when you click to.

## Light on your game

- Your position goes out a few times a minute while you move, and once a minute while you stand still. With Proximity voice on and a guildie in voice nearby, it goes every 3 seconds while you move, so voices follow smoothly.
- It's built and tested for a guild of 500-600 members with everyone running it.
- It uses about 2 MB of memory; Settings shows the figure.
- It tells you when a guildie has a newer version, so everyone stays up to date.

## Settings

- Choose what to share, switch each kind of pop-up on or off, pick the pop-up sound, and set the dot size, the ring colour and where dots show.
- Your alts in the guild link up automatically, so points and titles follow you, not just one character.

---

**Slash commands:** `/ynm` opens the window, `/ynm voice` turns proximity voice on or off (also `/ynm voice group`, `guild`, `leave`, or set a key under Keybindings > AddOns), `/ynm log` shows recent addon messages, `/ynm errors` shows any errors.
