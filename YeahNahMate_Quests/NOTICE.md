# Yeah Nah Mate Quests

Dungeon quest data for Yeah Nah Mate's leveling guide.

Quest data adapted from [Questie](https://github.com/Questie/Questie) (version 12.9.0) and QuestieDB
(version 1.1.1). Original work by the Questie contributors.

**What was changed (22 November 2026):** trimmed to the quests that belong to WoW Forever's dungeons and the
quests leading to them, cut down to each quest's name, levels, who can take it, who starts it, where, and
what comes before it, and reformatted for this addon. Crafting and tier turn-ins were removed.
